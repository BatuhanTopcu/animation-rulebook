# Style direction

Medium is how the image is made; style is how it behaves and communicates. A 3D
scene can use held poses and graphic smears, and a 2D scene can pursue naturalistic
timing. Choose a coherent treatment rather than equating 3D with smooth realism.

## Extract a usable style brief

Start with the user's references and existing project. Observe movement, not
only a poster or a still. Record the few traits that will affect decisions:

- **Cadence:** continuous samples, held poses, mixed exposures, or stop-motion.
- **Posing:** restrained, pushed silhouettes, limited replacements, asymmetry.
- **Timing:** flowing, abrupt, rhythmic, delayed, or punctuated by holds.
- **Material and energy:** stiffness, deformation allowance, rebound, damping.
- **Image language:** dimensional or flat, blur or smears, depth cheats, line boil.
- **Staging/camera:** observer, motivated tracking, graphic cuts, deliberate stillness.

Describe observable traits instead of relying on a style label alone. If only
stills are available, acknowledge that cadence and movement cannot be inferred
reliably from them. For a substantial uncertain treatment, make a small
representative motion test before extending the style to the full sequence.

## Profiles as starting points

| Direction | Decisions to emphasize | Do not impose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Naturalistic | Credible support, force, anatomy, modest irregularity, motivated gaze | Symmetrical easing and mathematically perfect movement everywhere |
| Exaggerated cartoon | Clear extremes, elastic or articulated posing, timing contrast, strong accents | Uniform squash or bounce on every material |
| Limited / anime-influenced | Strong keys, selective exposure changes, held information, designed action accents | A universal "anime fps" or interpolation of all holds |
| Stop-motion | Deliberate pose increments, material character, consistent contact, chosen texture of motion | Random jitter as a shortcut for handmade movement |
| Graphic / abstract | Legible shape relationships, purposeful rhythm, hierarchy, clean transformations | Physical realism when spatial symbolism is the goal |
| Hybrid 2D/3D | Consistent registration, purposeful differences in cadence, compatible accents | One exposure setting for all layers regardless of role |

Real projects can combine profiles. Decide which traits stay consistent and
where contrast serves the scene; do not assign conflicting defaults to the same
subject on adjacent beats without a reason.

## Exceptions should explain the result

A long airborne hold, stretched limb, impossible perspective, sudden snap, or
nonphysical camera can be correct for the brief. Make the audience's intended
reading the test. Preserve enough continuity around an accent that it reads as
a decision rather than an error.

An artistically flat composition need not follow solid-volume construction in
every frame. A rigid mechanical style may favor precise linear travel. A quiet
premium reveal may need no overshoot. Do not use those choices as universal
rules for all animation in the same medium.

## Mixed cadence needs composite review

Sony Imageworks describes using stepped animation on twos in *Into the
Spider-Verse* to achieve a graphic, illustrated quality. This demonstrates a
specific artistic choice, not a universal cadence for stylized 3D. See
[production source](https://www.imageworks.com/node/1371).

When layers or characters use different exposures, inspect relative motion,
contact registration, camera tracking, and blur at delivery speed. Decide whether
a parent transform remains continuous while pose changes step, or whether both
step; that choice depends on the intended look and contacts. Do not blindly
quantize every animation channel or smooth the full scene to repair one defect.

## Matching and refinement

Compare the same representative beat before and after changes. Evaluate silhouette,
timing contrast, material behavior, and cadence before decorative effects.
When matching a reference, preserve its defining traits while respecting the
requested subject and deliverable. Explicit user direction overrides these
starting profiles. Use [review](review.md) to separate an unintended defect from
a chosen imperfection.
